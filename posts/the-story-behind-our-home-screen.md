All children love books. They love looking at it even before they can read.

Being a book publisher, we share the same interest.

So when we decided to develop an educational app for kids, we wanted it to be around books. We also wanted to bring the way kids interact with books into StoryTime.

If you have a mini library for your kids at home, you might have noticed this.

Kids will throw their books on the floor just for the fun of it. Then they’ll come back to the piles of books on the floor, try to find a book, pick it up and start reading it.

That’s the whole concept behind our home screen.

![StoryTime Home Screen](https://ktsbzbfzsjqwrgpdrvrg.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog-images/the-story-behind-our-home-screen/image-1.webp)

Kids can choose which book (digital activity) they want to play with. They can move the book around and interact with it, just like in real life.

Books are important to kids. And through StoryTime we want kids to always be close to books even while they play on a screen.
