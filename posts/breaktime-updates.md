We’ve made a few major improvement in BreakTime.

Before, users were redirected to the app homepage, losing the last play state. This creates upsetting moment for kids when all their hardwork are lost especially in activity like Pixel and Shapes.

Now, after BreakTime ended users will be returned to the last activities they were playing.

Here are some more detailed explanation for each of the improvements we made:

![](https://ktsbzbfzsjqwrgpdrvrg.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog-images/breaktime-updates/image-1.webp)

Our goal in StoryTime is to provide calm, healthy and safe screen-time space for kids. We hope with these new improvement it's a step closer to make our goal a reality.
