StoryTime is not a game

StoryTime is not for kids to learn how to read

StoryTime is not for kids to learn how to calculate

StoryTime is not for kids to score exams

StoryTime is not for kids to be told what answer is right and what is wrong

StoryTime is not designed to replace books

StoryTime is not designed to replace playing outdoor

StoryTime is not designed to replace real human connection

StoryTime is not to be played for a long time

StoryTime is not for kids under 2 years old

StoryTime is not the place to collect scores and stars

StoryTime is a set of digital activities to **explore** and **discover** many things. And while using it, your kids will learn about **emotional regulation**. They might **love** our beautiful earth more. They might improve their **focus** and **attention** and they will learn how to **think better**.
