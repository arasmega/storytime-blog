Without doubt one of the concerns of every parent is our kids' ability to read. While “ABCs” is not designed to teach kids how to read, we designed it to teach kids the sounds of each alphabet. Which is essential for kids to know before they can start reading.

In StoryTime, one of our principles is to not include instruction on how to play the activities. This is part of our effort to celebrate kids' natural curiosity. “ABCs” gameplay has been made simple so that kids can explore and discover how to play on their own. 

In “ABCs” we use simple sounds and objects that kids can easily understand. Each alphabet will transform into an object that starts with that particular alphabet.
