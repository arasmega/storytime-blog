When we started building StoryTime last year, we decided to focus on the iPhone first then the iPad. Our reasoning was, that more people use iPhones than iPads.

How wrong we were.

Since we launched in November last year (2023), we saw half of the downloads are on iPad. Yes, you can use StoryTime on iPad, but it is the scale-up iPhone view, not the full iPad view.

That tells us a very important message, we need to add iPad version on the App Store as soon as possible.

For the past month, that's exactly what we did. Our team have been working hard to add iPad version to StoryTime alongside our next Digital Activity, Balance.

You can now [**update**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/storytime-digital-activities/id6446242246) your iPad app to get the full iPad experience.

Thank you for your support.
